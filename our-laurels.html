    LeadershipNational7 May 2007

    Source: School History: At Rashtrapati Bhavan

    International · The SchoolInternational2011

    The International School Award

    British Council

    CIRS received the British Council’s International School Award, which recognises good practice in bringing an international dimension into the curriculum. The Junior School had taken up the programme the year before, with holiday assignments on the wider world.

    Source: School History: The International School Award

  4. 2012

    The School2012

    Source: School History: Brainfeed Top 500 Schools of India

    Source: School History: Education World survey
