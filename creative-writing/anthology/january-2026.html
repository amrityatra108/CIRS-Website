Ved Tulsyan

Grade 11 · Management

Link to this poem ↗

The joy of little things, From the sound of the bat to hearing the birds sing You never know what life brings.

Life’s too short to complain. From unnecessary sorrows, you must refrain.

It is those tiny tiny moments As primitive as watching a comet. That fits your heart with bliss. And that is what is the joy of little things.

04 / 16 / Untitled poem

Arnav Saraf

Grade 11 · Management

Link to this poem ↗

One late noon, it was cloud’s day, The sky dark and the feeling gay. Few soldiers fight in the battlefield, While the king plays with no guilt.

I look at them and tell my lads, “You are living your lives”. Wish we’d be out of this mess, To relive these times with complete bliss.

On a nearby puddle they play, With their rides and swords they prey. Slaying weach other and their guards, And splashing the elixir on our lords.

The battle continues with screams But for us, slaves, we only live with dreams. The joy of these little things that we see at night Are what we wish to attain with all our might.

05 / 16 / Untitled poem

Jishnu Teja Yogesh

IB year 1

Link to this poem ↗

As I take a stroll down the streets, Tiny pearls of water greet. A sound surrounded, not upbeat, But a melodious and euphonious tweet.

They gather softly on my sleeve Like secrets the drizzle chose to leave Magically, the day feels light The ripples gather below gently but with might.

No glory, no big claim No sun, no blazing flame. The chaos inside me drowned By the king but uncrowned.

Nature roars beyond my sight Covered by this subtle delight But in a drop that kissed my hand, I found more delight than he had ever planned

14 / 16 / Untitled poem

Anushya UK

IB year 1

Link to this poem ↗

The joy of little things is not waking up happy every day, but finding small reasons to smile anyway.

Like getting an extra chocolate at the bottom of the bag, or when your favorite song plays right when you needed it.

It’s laughing over dumb jokes that don’t even make sense later, and sitting quietly with someone enjoying the silence.

It’s the smell of rain, the feeling when warm sunlight peeps in through the window, and watching the same old movies because it somehow feels comfortable.

We keep thinking joy has to be big, like something special or planned, but most of the time it’s just hidden in simple moments.

And maybe that’s why it matters— because the little things, don’t try to impress you. They just show up.
