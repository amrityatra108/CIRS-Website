Untitled · first line

Where I reside right now,

Kavya Arun Prasad

Where I reside right now,Is not where I want to be.Scared and alone,In a world not as care-free.

Responsibilities and worries,Exist as live entities.Troubling my mind constantly,Sometimes getting me on my knees.

And I yearn with my heart,A place where I can sleep.The softness and warmth of the security,A long rest without a peep.

So I go running back,Blind, without clarity.To my family, a warmth I’ll forever seek,The one’s who I call, my refuge from reality.

Untitled · first line

Reality is not something easy

Adira Chellam P

Reality is not something easyIt might make you go crazyIf I had to take a break from mineI’ll run to the place which’ll make me shine.

Anytime to escape this toxic danger,I neither have to meet my friends nor a stranger.I just have to rush towards that roomWhere my dance skills will unleash and bloom.

Its a place where I forget everythingI dance to my fullest and sometimes also sing.This is my refuge, my way out,I’ll dance forever with no doubt.

03 / 03

Untitled · first line

When the whole world gets way too loud,

Tarushi agarwal

When the whole world gets way too loud,And I just hate being in the crowd,I open my drawer and grab a page,To let go of all my stress and rage.

With a HB pencil in my hand,I create my own quiet land.No marks, no tests, no one to judge,Just black lines and a charcoal smudge.

This notebook is my silent space,My favorite little hiding place.Here I don't have to explain a thing,I just draw until my thoughts take wing.
