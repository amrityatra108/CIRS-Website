How to apply

Six Stages, in Order.

From the first enquiry to a confirmed place.

Admission enquiry

Write or call the admissions office with any question about the year applied for, the examination centres or a visit. Nothing is committed at this stage.

admissions@cirschool.org
WhatsApp +91 93604 61572
+91 422 261 3300 · 261 3303

Registration

Complete every mandatory field before submitting. The student’s name must match the school records exactly, and the date of birth must match the birth certificate issued by the competent authority. Give a valid email address — the entrance examination details are sent to it. Then pay the registration fee online; cash is not accepted.

₹5,000
Online payment only
Portal closes 15 October 2026

Application assessment

The school reviews each submitted application. Registration alone does not confer a right to admission.

By the school
Before the examination

Entrance examination

The paper is general and is not based on any one board or syllabus. It is set to assess what a candidate has learnt so far and how readily they will learn next, across widely varied curricula. Every paper includes a creative writing section.

First week of November 2026
Centres in India, Dubai and other countries
See the structure

Admissions interview

Candidates who progress are called for interview with the school.

By invitation
Following the examination

Confirmation, and fees

Successful candidates are notified, and the place is confirmed on payment of fees. Caste certificates for OBC, SC and ST candidates are required at this point.

Visiting
