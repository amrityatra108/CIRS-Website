The complete archive

All milestones.

24 records in date order, each with its source. Choose one to read it in full.

  1. 1970s · Before opening

    An international school is imagined

    The idea of starting an international school in India was conceived by Pujya Gurudev Swami Chinmayananda, and it met with an overwhelming response from all over the world. Bangalore, Lucknow, the Andamans and Himachal Pradesh were each evaluated before Coimbatore was chosen.

    Source The school’s published history, cirschool.org

  2. 1984 · Before opening

    One rupee at a time

    Swami Sahayanandaji travelled the length and breadth of India on foot, making the initial collection towards the purchase of the land: one rupee from each individual.

    Source The school’s published history, cirschool.org

  3. 3 August 1993 · Before opening

    Gurudev attains Mahasamadhi

    Pujya Gurudev Swami Chinmayananda attained Mahasamadhi on 3 August 1993. He did not see the school he had conceived open its doors.

    Source The chronology of Gurudev’s life on this site’s Founder page

  4. 1994 · Before opening

    The vision is made concrete

    Pujya Guruji Swami Tejomayananda proceeded to concretise the vision of Pujya Gurudev with unfailing vigour and energy, and the work progressed by leaps and bounds.

    Source The school’s published history, cirschool.org

  5. Until 1996 · Before opening

    Guided to completion

    Many devotees around the world contributed generously. Swamini Vimalanandaji and Dr. G. S. Keshawamurthy played a significant role in guiding and executing the project to its completion.

    Source The school’s published history, cirschool.org

  6. 6 June 1996 · Early years

    The school is inaugurated

    The Chinmaya International Residential School was inaugurated by Pujya Swami Chidanandaji, President of The Divine Life Society. The plaque at the main building records that he did so in the presence of Swami Tejomayananda, Head of Chinmaya Mission, on Thursday, 6 June 1996.

    Source The school’s published history, cirschool.org; the inauguration plaque at the main building

  7. June 1996 · Early years

    Ninety-six students, eleven teachers

    CIRS started with 96 students from Grade V to Grade VIII and 11 academic staff, headed by Dr. Jaya Venugopal, with Brahmacharini Sumati Chaitanya and Brahmachari Samahita Chaitanya as spiritual guides.

    Source The school’s published history, cirschool.org

  8. 15 July 1996 · Early years

    The State’s no-objection

    The Director of School Education, Government of Tamil Nadu, wrote to the Central Board of Secondary Education that the department had no objection to the syllabus of the middle classes at CIRS being affiliated to the Board, subject to one condition: Tamil should be taught as a second language.

    Source No Objection Certificate, published on School Information · Open the PDF

  9. 2005 · Early years

    A Resident Director

    Pujya Guruji appointed Swami Swaroopanandaji as Resident Director of CIRS.

    Source The school’s own account, supplied for this website

  10. 7 May 2007 · Early years

    At Rashtrapati Bhavan

    Thirty-two students from Classes VI to XII, with one alumnus, four teachers and Principal Anurag Sangal, met President A. P. J. Abdul Kalam in the Committee Room of Rashtrapati Bhavan. The invitation grew out of a vacation assignment set in November 2006 on the President’s ‘Vision for India 2020’, which the school had recast for its students as ‘My Vision for Myself, my Family, my School and my Country – in 2020’. Selected projects were sent to the President, and led to his interest in meeting them.

    Source The school’s account of the interaction, kept from its former website

  11. May 2008 · Early years

    A newsletter the students made

    Chinmaya Sakshi, the school’s e-newsletter, was put together by the students’ editorial team: they gathered the news, edited it and designed the layout, ‘with a minimal assistance and guidance from the teachers’, as the Principal’s note in the October–November 2007 edition puts it. The summer special of May 2008 opens on the campus beneath the hills and a portrait of Gurudev.

    Source Chinmaya Sakshi, October–November 2007 and May 2008 editions, kept from the school’s former website

  12. 2009 · Early years

    A new Principal

    Smt. Shanti Krishnamurthy became Principal of CIRS.

    Source The school’s introduction on Leadership

  13. August 2010 · Early years

    Reflections, the IB newsletter

    Reflections, the newsletter of the school’s IB Diploma students, reached its fourth issue with a special on the first-year students’ tour of Sri Lanka in August 2010 — ‘a blend of the experience of a new culture and service to humanity’, in the words of the IB Diploma Coordinator.

    Source Reflections, Volume 1, Issue 4, kept from the school’s former website

  14. December 2010 · Early years

    The world in the curriculum

    The Junior School’s December 2010 holiday assignment was set as part of the British Council’s International School Award programme, which CIRS had taken up to bring an international dimension into its curriculum. Class V studied tourist attractions across the world, Class VI music across the globe, Class VII national games, and Class VIII agricultural practices.

    Source The assignment sheet, kept from the school’s former website

  15. 2011 · Later milestones

    The International School Award

    CIRS received the British Council’s International School Award, which recognises good practice in bringing an international dimension into the curriculum.

    Source The school’s own account, supplied for this website; its former website carried a gallery titled ‘CIRS Receives ISA Award’

  16. 2012 · Later milestones

    The CCMT Education Cell Vision Award

    The Education Cell of the Central Chinmaya Mission Trust presented its Vision Award for 2012 to CIRS, Coimbatore.

    Source The inscription on the award

  17. 2014 · Later milestones

    A partnership with a school in Poland

    From February 2014 a CIRS class worked with an upper-secondary and technical college in Poland on a shared project, ‘Trends in advertising: the impact of language and culture on advertising’, exchanging videos, worksheets and advertisements of their own.

    Source The school’s newsletter page on the partnership, May 2014, kept from its former website

  18. 12 November 2017 · Later milestones

    Brainfeed Top 500 Schools of India

    Brainfeed’s School Excellence Awards named CIRS among the Top 500 Schools of India 2017–18, in the category Best International Boarding / Happiness Quotient Index / Influential School Brand School, and among the best international schools of Tamil Nadu. The award was presented in Bengaluru.

    Source The plaque itself, photographed for the school

  19. 2018 · Later milestones

    Director — Academics and Administration

    Smt. Shanti Krishnamurthy became Director — Academics and Administration.

    Source The school’s introduction on Leadership

  20. 15 October 2019 · Later milestones

    The school in its twenty-fourth year

    In its 24th year the school reported 580 students — 345 boys and 235 girls — from 22 Indian states and 18 other countries, with 67 faculty members and 68 members of the administrative team. That year the CBSE selected CIRS as one of its Lead Schools, to guide five other CBSE schools, and Sanskrit became a compulsory subject, beginning in Class V.

    Source Annual Report, 15 October 2019, published on School Information · Open the PDF

  21. 2019 · Later milestones

    Education World survey

    In Education World’s 2019 survey, conducted by the Delhi-based C fore, CIRS moved from fourth to second place in the country among co-educational boarding schools, and kept first place in Tamil Nadu for the eighth consecutive year.

    Source The school’s Annual Report, 15 October 2019 · Open the PDF

  22. 2019 · Later milestones

    Class XII results, 2019

    All 45 students who sat the Class XII examination secured above 80% in the aggregate, and 41 of them above 90%. The Science stream averaged 92.06% and the Management stream 94.23%.

    Source The school’s Annual Report, 15 October 2019 · Our Results

  23. March–April 2026 · Later milestones

    CBSE Class XII, 2026

    In the 2026 CBSE Class XII examinations the highest score in the Management stream was 99.0%, and the stream averaged 94%.

    Source The school’s results brief on News

  24. May 2026 · Later milestones

    Twenty-four candidates, twenty-four diplomas

    All 24 candidates in the May 2026 IB Diploma examination earned the diploma, with an average of 39.4 points. Two students scored the maximum, 45 out of 45.

    Source The school’s results brief on News

The complete record of examination results and honours is kept on Our Results and Our Laurels.
