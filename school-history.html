The origin

A School Imagined Before It Had a Place.

The idea of an international school in India was conceived by Pujya Gurudev Swami Chinmayananda. Sites were weighed in Bangalore, Lucknow, the Andamans and Himachal Pradesh before Coimbatore was chosen — and the hundred acres in the Siruvani foothills were bought one rupee at a time.

In 1979 the Coimbatore Chinmaya Mission Committee found the land, and Pujya Gurudev called it ‘the Sidhbari of the South’. From 1984 the money to buy it was collected on foot, one rupee from every household. After Gurudev’s Mahasamadhi in 1993, Pujya Guruji Swami Tejomayananda took the project upon his own shoulders and carried it, as the school’s own account has it, with unfailing vigour and energy.

On 6 June 1996 the Chinmaya International Residential School was inaugurated by Pujya Swami Chidanandaji, President of The Divine Life Society. It opened with ninety-six students in Grades V to VIII and eleven academic staff.

The first school was led by Dr. Jaya Venugopal, with Brahmacharini Sumati Chaitanya and Brahmachari Samahita Chaitanya as its spiritual guides, and with Swamini Vimalanandaji and Dr. G. S. Keshawamurthy among those who carried it into being.

The school’s own history records its growth to 577 students, 69 faculty and 78 staff, drawn from 23 Indian states and 19 other countries. [Current figures to be confirmed by the school.]

The milestones

From a Vision to a Campus.

Nearly fifty years, from the day the land was found to this year’s results. Scroll to follow the line; rest on a year to bring it forward.

  1. The founding

  2. 1979

    The land is found

    The Coimbatore Chinmaya Mission Committee, guided by A. Somasundaram, identifies the hundred acres that will become the campus. Pujya Gurudev proclaims the site ‘the Sidhbari of the South’ — and that the school would be built there and nowhere else.

  3. 1984

    One rupee at a time

    Pujya Gurudev and Br. Sahaja Chaitanyaji begin a grassroots programme to collect one rupee from every household, on foot. It takes another ten years to procure all of the land the school needs.

  4. 1993

    The project is carried on

    Pujya Gurudev attains Mahasamadhi. The indefatigable Swami Tejomayanandaji takes the project upon his own shoulders.

  5. 1996

    The school is inaugurated

    CIRS is inaugurated by Swami Chidanandaji Maharaj, head of The Divine Life Society and Gurudev’s dearest friend, in the presence of Pujya Guruji Swami Tejomayanandaji. Swamini Vimalanandaji becomes Director of CIRS. Gurudev’s sankalpa has been fulfilled.

  6. The school grows

  7. 2005

    A resident director

    Pujya Guruji appoints Swami Swaroopanandaji as Resident Director of CIRS.

  8. 2009

    A new Principal

    Smt. Shanti Krishnamurthy takes over as Principal of CIRS.

  9. 2011

    The International School Award

    CIRS receives the British Council International School Award, recognising its international education initiatives.

  10. 2013

    The Chinmaya Vision Award

    CIRS receives the Chinmaya Vision Award.

  11. 2016

    Among the top four

    Education World ranks CIRS among the top four institutions in the country.

  12. 2018

    Second in the country, first in CBSE

    CIRS rises through the ranks to second place, and ranks first in the CBSE Board results.

  13. 2018

    Director — Academics & Administration

    Smt. Shanti Krishnamurthy becomes Director — Academics and Administration.

  14. Recent years

  15. 2019

    Among the IB World Toppers

    A CIRS student is among the IB World Toppers, with a score of 45 out of 45.

  16. 2019

    The first CIRSMUN

    The first CIRS Model United Nations is conducted.

  17. 2022

    A 92% average in Science

    CIRS reports a 92% average in the Science stream in the Class XII Board Examinations, with 19 of 24 students scoring 90% or above.

  18. 2024

    IB World Toppers, once more

    A CIRS student is once more among the IB World Toppers, setting the standard for generations to come.

  19. 2025

    First prize for Vayu Nigrah

    Three CIRS students win first prize in the Studentrepreneur competition at the SSVM Transforming India Conclave for Vayu Nigrah, a project that generates electricity from air-conditioning systems.

  20. 2026

    99% and two perfect scores

    The highest score in the CBSE Management stream is 99%, and two students achieve the highest possible IB score of 45 out of 45.
