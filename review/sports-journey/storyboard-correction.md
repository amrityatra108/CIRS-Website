# Our Sports — correction storyboard

Prepared from the supplied film and CIRS action photographs before implementation. The [browser review](browser-review.md) records the built result.

## The opening and MOVE handoff

| State | Composition | Motion decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | The film's exact final frame fills the screen: ball low at center, white field line, sunrise and Ghats. A small “Scroll to discover” cue belongs to the opening film. | Hold the final frame for only the first beat of the next scene. Do not let another copy appear below the film. |
| Action | `0C9A5528.JPG` runners arrive along the track line, revealing the full photo across the field frame. | Let the track line carry one soft, fast wipe. Finish the image reveal before MOVE reaches poster scale. |
| Hold | One sharp, full-screen crop of the same race photo sits behind a centered gold MOVE. | Hold the letters steady and readable over a single image. No split frame or second photograph enters behind the word. |
| Exit | `0C9A5453.JPG` opens into a wider finish view along the white lane. MOVE changes to purple with a gold edge and the short Athletics line arrives at lower left. | MOVE travels up and left while it scales down; the finish photo takes over in one continuous reveal. |

## After the signature transition

The pinned photo sequence keeps existing Basketball and Water scenes, then moves through field hockey (`IMG_2336.JPG`), tennis (`IMG_2205.JPG`), a user-supplied basketball game (`CRS02091.JPG`), table tennis (`IMG_1564.JPG`), strength training (`IMG_1578.JPG`), a solo basketball drive (`CRS02296.JPG`) and the team huddle (`CRS01020.JPG`). Every scene uses a different photograph. The full-bleed yoga image remains the quieter pause before the grounds copy. Full source mapping is in [asset-sources.md](asset-sources.md).
