# Sports image provenance

All four school photographs below come from the CIRS sports Drive folder supplied for this page. The JPEG originals identify a Canon EOS 6D Mark II and a 2023-09-26 capture date in their EXIF metadata. The served WebP files are 1920×1200 art-directed landscape crops and 900×1950 portrait crops. GPS and camera metadata are omitted from the derivatives. The field frame is extracted from PR #46’s `assets/video/sports-field.mp4`, frame 120, and is the exact opening composition of the handoff scene.
