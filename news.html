AchievementsFinal examination / May 2026

Twenty-four candidates.
Twenty-four diplomas.

Every candidate registered for the May 2026 IB Diploma examination was awarded the diploma. The average total was 39.4 points; the highest was 45 out of 45.

Twelve students scored 40 points or above, eight scored between 36 and 39, and four scored below 36. Adithyan Diwakar Vidya and Shriya Shruti Misra were the toppers, both with 45 out of 45.

These are the school-reported results for the May 2026 final examination.

Class XII / Science

Sonakshi Birmiwal and Namburu Venkata Dheeraj topped with 95.60%. The class average was 91%; 12 students scored 90% or above and seven scored between 80% and 90%. There was one centum in Chemistry and two in IP.

Class XII / Management

Dhruvik Sachin Agarwal topped with 99.0%. The class average was 94%; 22 students scored 90% or above and four scored between 80% and 90%. There was one centum in Business Studies and two in Applied Mathematics.

Class X

Vishv Prem Nangia topped with 98.60%. The class average was 90%; 49 students scored 90% or above, 24 scored between 80% and 90%, and six scored below 80%. Centums were reported in Sanskrit (five), Hindi (two) and French (three).
