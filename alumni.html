Many paths. One beginning.

CIRS has been teaching on the Siruvani campus since 1996. What follows is where its students went after it — every institution the school has named, and every alumnus it has named, and nothing that it has not.

Five ways out of the same gate.

University counselling begins in Grade X and continues through Naviance. The IB opens direct application to North America, the UK, Europe and Australia; CBSE keeps the Indian national pathway fully open. Each pathway below names what it was drawn from.

1 / 5

Higher education abroad

The Diploma applies directly.

The IB opens direct application to North America, the UK, Europe and Australia. Fifteen of the nineteen institutions CIRS students have gone on to are outside India.

Drawn from

The Indian national pathway

And the national pathway stays open.

CBSE keeps the Indian route fully open, with board examinations at Grades X and XII and a choice of Engineering, Medicine and Management streams in the senior years.
