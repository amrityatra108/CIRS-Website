The houses of CIRS

Four Houses.
One CIRS.

Different colours. Shared purpose.

Choose a house — or keep scrolling

Vasishtha

01 / 04

Vasishtha

Red ·The lion

Just like the lion, our house symbolizes strength and determination, never giving up and a readiness to break new challenges.

Vasishtha House, writing in Sakshi, February 2008

Vasishtha was the first house to pass a thousand points in the school’s history, and in February 2008 it described itself as the unbeaten champion of the march past. Its drills have used pompoms and poles, and before those, pyramids, umbrellas and leziums.

Vasishtha in frames

Valmiki

02 / 04

Valmiki

Yellow ·The tiger

We perceive the goal through our all-seeing eye; that of a tiger, and we hope to continue our remarkable tradition of victory.

Valmiki House, writing in Sakshi, February 2008

Valmiki took both the overall championship and the best march past at Khel Mela 2008, and won the Senior Social Science Quiz in April that year. Its Khel Mela drill that season was a dupatta and cap drill.

Valmiki in frames

Vishwamitra

03 / 04

Vishwamitra

Green ·The eagle

A fierce and elegant eagle, soaring through cloudy night skies and bright days, soaring through life.

Vishwamitra House, writing in Sakshi, February 2008

The eagle has been Vishwamitra’s symbol since 2005. The house won the Inter-House Aquatic Meet and the junior English quiz in the second term of 2008, and brought the fight drill — two armies, staged as a series of exercises — to Khel Mela for the first time.

Vishwamitra in frames

Vyasa

04 / 04

Vyasa

Blue ·The dragon

Winning is difficult, but not impossible, and this is the hope that keeps us striving for the impossible.

Vyasa House, writing in Sakshi, February 2008

Vyasa took the best drill cup at Khel Mela 2008 with a drill of hoops and a bamboo dance, and it won the Junior Science Quiz that November.

Vyasa in frames

The march past

Colour.
Discipline.
Pride.

The march past is judged, and it is won. Vasishtha called itself the unbeaten champion of it in 2008; Valmiki took it that February. It is the one event where a house is only as good as its quietest rank.
