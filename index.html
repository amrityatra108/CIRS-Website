A DAY ONE HUNDRED ACRES FROM FIRST LIGHT UNTIL DARK

A day at CIRS

ONE HUNDRED ACRES,
FROM FIRST LIGHT

The day starts outdoors and finishes under the amphitheatre lights. Between those two hours are the classrooms and the drums, the table, the water and the trail — and nowhere on the hundred acres to be a spectator.

Jñānam · Knowledge

HELD UP
TO THE LIGHT

A flask raised at the front of a laboratory; a rhythm counted out until the hands have it. Grades V to XII, taught towards CBSE and the IB — and, just as deliberately, towards the habit of asking.

Sevā · Service

A HALL, AND
THEN A TABLE

Boarding is the part of this school that no timetable shows. What the Chinmaya Vision Programme names as values is not a period in the week here; it is the ordinary business of living on the same hundred acres.

Kauśalam · Efficiency

DONE PROPERLY,
OR NOT AT ALL

The third word of the motto is the one most often mistranslated as speed. It means skill — the slope walked to the top of it, the piece rehearsed until it holds, the length finished.

A Hundred Acres, in a Hundred Frames.

Every photograph here was taken on this campus — the classrooms and the laboratories, the amphitheatre, the parades, the stage and the water. The wall keeps moving, and every frame is dissolving into another. Point at one to stop the band and bring its colour back.
