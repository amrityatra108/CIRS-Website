A day at CIRS

ONE HUNDRED ACRES,
FROM FIRST LIGHT

The day starts on the field and finishes under the amphitheatre lights. Every student walks the knife’s edge of balance, endlessly engaged in activity or immersed in silence.

Jñānam · Knowledge

HELD UP
TO THE LIGHT

A flask raised at the front of a laboratory; a rhythm flowing through the artist. Grades V to XII, taught towards CBSE and the IB — and, just as deliberately, towards the inquisitive nature of the human mind.

Sevā · Service

AT HIS FEET,
THROUGH THEM

Every action is oriented not toward personal reward, but toward gratitude and service. An offer of help, a hand around a shoulder, a word of advice: one act goes a long way in bringing about change.

Kauśalam · Efficiency

DONE PROPERLY,
OR NOT AT ALL

Kaushalam means skill — the slope walked to the top of it, the piece rehearsed to perfection, the length finished.
