Four grade divisions

Find your challenge.

The challenge runs in four divisions, from Grade 5 to Grade 12. Choose yours to open the winners’ bulletins the mathematics department has published for it.

  1. 01 Grades 5–6 View published results

    Number sense, pattern and logic — problems that reward noticing rather than calculating quickly.

    7 winners’ bulletins in the archive

  2. 02 Grades 7–8 View published results

    Reasoning stretched further: relationships, geometry and the kind of puzzle that needs a plan before a pencil.

    7 winners’ bulletins in the archive

  3. 03 Grades 9–10 View published results

    Problems that ask a student to connect ideas from different parts of the syllabus, and to justify the connection.

    6 winners’ bulletins in the archive

  4. 04 Grades 11–12 View published results

    Sustained problems of the sort that reward a whole evening, and the habits of mind that higher study asks for.

    5 winners’ bulletins in the archive

Five stages

How a mind solves.

  1. 01

    Question

    Read it twice. Decide what is actually being asked, and what is only decoration.

  2. 02

    Think

    Look for the pattern, the symmetry, the thing that stays the same while everything else moves.

  3. 03

    Explore

    Try the small case. Draw it. Guess, then test the guess and find out why it failed.

  4. 04

    Solve

    Build the argument step by step, and check that each step follows from the one before it.

  5. 05

    Discover

    Ask what the problem was really about — that is the part that carries to the next one.
