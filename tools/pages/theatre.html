CIRS Theatre

Every Student, On the Stage.

For over three decades CIRS has been a whirlpool of theatrical activity — and the stage is not kept for a drama club. Every class, every house and the whole school take their turn on it.

Every year the school churns out actors whose qualities are learnt on the stage and blossom into daily life. Confidence, expression and creativity find no limits here. Every novice aspires to the honourable title of ‘actor’, and theatre is always treated as sacred.

Programmes are put up to bring messages from our texts, stories from our students, and to celebrate the art of expression itself — with the concepts, the scripts and the acting done almost entirely by the students, on the stage and off it.
