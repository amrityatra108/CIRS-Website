A Campus That Never Really Closes.

School life

Sixteen hours, lived together.

A boarding school is a schedule. Scroll through the one the whole campus keeps.

05:30

Prayer and chanting

The day begins in the prayer hall while the valley is still dark — the same fifteen minutes for a Grade V boarder and a Grade XII Diploma candidate.

Before the mountain wakes.

08:15

Academics

CBSE from Grade V, the IB Diploma from Grade XI — CBSE subject teachers and IB-trained specialists, both in English.

Two boards, two trained faculties.

16:00

Games

Athletics, football, cricket, basketball, swimming and yoga. Eighty of the hundred acres are left wild, so the boundary of play is the edge of the Ghats.

The forest is the touchline.

18:00

Music, dance and theatre

A veena class ends as a band sets up; a Bharatanatyam rehearsal shares the wing with a drama read-through.

Both traditions are native here.

19:30

House meetings

Rosters, service projects and the week’s disputes. Seniors are accountable for juniors by name, not by rota.

The hour that makes leaders.

21:00

Prep ends, dormitories settle

The closing chant carries across the valley. Six hundred students from twenty countries end the day on the same syllable.

One silence, six hundred people.
