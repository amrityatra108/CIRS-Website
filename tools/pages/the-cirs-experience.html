Life together

A campus that never really stops.

Dormitories, classrooms, the Annakshetra dining hall and playing fields form one walkable campus. Meals are shared in the dining hall, and the day’s classes, study, games and prayer all happen a short walk from where students live.

Spiritual class and assembly

After hot milk and biscuits, spiritual class brings Bhagavad Gita shlokas, stotram and japa into the day. Breakfast at the Annakshetra leads to assembly, where the Principal addresses the school and students regularly offer talks, speeches, book reviews and quizzes.

Residential life

Residential houses are named for great figures of Bharat’s history. Questions about boarding, visits and what a new student brings are answered by the Admissions Office.

Amphitheatre and facilities

The amphitheatre is built into the slope, with the hills behind the stage and music rising from the audience. The campus also includes laboratories, a library and sports facilities.
