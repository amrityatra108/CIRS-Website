Since 1996

Ninety-six students, and a hundred acres.

Chinmaya International Residential School was conceived by Pujya Gurudev Swami Chinmayananda and inaugurated on 6 June 1996, three years after his mahasamadhi, opening with ninety-six students. It is an undertaking of the Central Chinmaya Mission Trust.

The motto is taught as a daily practice rather than displayed as a crest. CBSE and the IB Diploma have run side by side since the beginning, each taught by teachers trained in that curriculum.

The campus

A decision that cost buildable land.

Roughly eighty of the campus’s hundred acres have been left to native flora and fauna. It cost the school land it could have built on, and earned it the Green School Award from the Centre for Science and Environment, New Delhi.

The school sits in the Siruvani foothills outside Coimbatore, and takes boarders from twenty-four Indian states and twenty countries.

Our motto

Knowledge, service and skill.

kaṭibaddhā vayaṁ sarve lakṣyaṁ sādhayituṁ varam | śikṣāyāstrividhaṁ yadvai jñānaṁ sevā ca kauśalam ||

The motto holds that knowledge is gained in order to serve the world efficiently. It asks for continual learning towards a positive impact; for service offered with compassion and dedication; and for efficiency, because efficiency is what turns good intent into contribution.

The three together, not one of them alone.

Boarding

Mentoring is structural, not extracurricular.

Houses carry the names of India’s great teachers, and seniors and juniors share corridors by design.

Boarders arrive from twenty-four Indian states and twenty countries and share a single dining hall, a single timetable and a single set of house rosters. Difference is not a programme here — it is the corridor.

The vision

Unlocking potential with care.

An individual’s unique perspective, shaped by personal values, is key to nurturing morally upright citizens who can serve society effectively. Education aims for holistic growth, blending academic proficiency with character development, and CIRS provides an enriching environment for students to excel.

The approach is to develop competent, sensitive and ethical young people, and to unlock the potential of exceptional children until they are conscientious global citizens. Safety and holistic well-being come first, and the strategies for both are revised continually rather than settled once.

CIRS teaches through the Chinmaya Vision Programme, which integrates culture, philosophy and academics. The programme sets out to equip future citizens to meet challenges positively, and asks children, school management, teachers and parents each to contribute to that.

Senior School

Two boards, one ambition.

From Grade XI students declare a direction — CBSE’s Engineering, Medicine or Management streams, or the IB Diploma, where every teacher is IB-trained and several serve as IB examiners.

The Diploma runs across six subject groups, three or four of them at Higher Level, anchored by the Extended Essay, Theory of Knowledge and CAS — recognised by universities in India and worldwide.

Junior School

A broad first exposure.

The junior years begin in Grade V, introducing boarding life, house mentorship, and a broad first exposure to music, sport and the sciences alongside the CBSE curriculum. Value education runs alongside the timetable from Bal Bhagavatam onward.
