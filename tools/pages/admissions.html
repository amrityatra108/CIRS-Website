Why families choose CIRS

Rooted in Values,
Ready for the World.

This page is for parents who want their child to grow into a capable citizen of a widening world without being cut loose from the civilisation they come from.

CIRS teaches through the Chinmaya Vision Programme, which does not treat character as something added once the syllabus is finished. A student’s mental and physical energies are directed together; pride in the culture they inherit is taught closely enough to be understood rather than merely performed; and both are held inside a regard for all living beings that keeps belonging from narrowing into something smaller.

The school sits in the Siruvani foothills outside Coimbatore and teaches CBSE and the IB Diploma Programme on one campus. This guide links to the application portal and published fee document, and helps families ask the Admissions Office for the details that apply to their child.

Application information

Check the current checklist.

Document requirements should come from the current application form or the Admissions Office. Use these questions before you submit.

Before opening the form

  • Confirm the intended entry year and class with Admissions
  • Ask which identity, birth and previous-school records the form requires
  • Confirm whether an examination centre must be selected
  • Check which email address and phone number the school should use

Before submitting

  • Check the current portal for its required fields and uploads
  • Ask Admissions whether any additional records apply to your child

For applicants outside India, confirm any travel or visa documentation directly with the school. Do not assume a previous year’s checklist applies.
