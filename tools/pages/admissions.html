Why families choose CIRS

Rooted in Values,
Ready for the World.

This page is for parents who want their child to grow into a capable citizen of a widening world without being cut loose from the civilisation they come from.

CIRS teaches through the Chinmaya Vision Programme, which does not treat character as something added once the syllabus is finished. A student’s mental and physical energies are directed together; pride in the culture they inherit is taught closely enough to be understood rather than merely performed; and both are held inside a regard for all living beings that keeps belonging from narrowing into something smaller.

The school sits on a hundred acres in the Siruvani foothills outside Coimbatore and takes boarders through CBSE and the IB Diploma Programme on one campus. What follows is the application process, the entrance examination, the fee document, how to arrange a visit, and who to ask.

Parent questions

Useful answers before you apply.

These answers use only the process and contact information currently published by the school.

Which classes can families currently apply for?

The current admissions information lists Classes V–IX and XI, with CBSE and the IB Diploma Programme identified for Class XI.

Is CIRS a residential school?

Yes. CIRS is a residential school in the Siruvani foothills near Coimbatore. Specific boarding arrangements for a candidate should be confirmed with Admissions.

How do we apply?

Registration is completed through the external application portal. Every mandatory field must be completed and the ₹5,000 registration fee is paid online.

Open the application portal

What does the entrance examination cover?

The examination is general and not based on one board. Classes V–VI take English and Mathematics; Classes VII–IX add Science; Class XI papers depend on the chosen CBSE stream or the IB Diploma route. Every paper includes creative writing.

Where can we find the fee information?

The published Schedule of Fee and Charges for 2027–2029 is available above as a PDF. Confirm its applicability with Admissions before making a financial decision.

Can international applicants use the same process?

The current information says entrance examination centres are available in India, Dubai and other countries. International document and visa requirements are not listed; contact Admissions for the requirements that apply.

Can our family visit the school?

Visits are available by appointment on Wednesdays and Saturdays. Visitors should reach the campus by 10.00 am sharp. Visitor numbers are limited and the school’s confirmation email must be shown at the entrance.
