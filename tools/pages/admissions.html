How to apply

Four Steps, in Order.

The whole process, from the form to the offer.

Fill in the registration form

Complete every mandatory field before submitting. The student’s name must match the school records exactly, and the date of birth must match the birth certificate issued by the competent authority. Give a valid email address — the entrance examination details are sent to it.

Pay the registration fee

You are guided to online payment after the completed form is submitted. Cash payments are not accepted.

Sit the entrance examination

The paper is general and is not based on any one board or syllabus. It is set to assess what a candidate has learnt so far and how readily they will learn next, across widely varied curricula. Every paper includes a creative writing section.
