CIRS Art Attack

Made by Hand.

Painting and drawing, craft and handwork, and the things made for the school’s own stage — the visual arts at CIRS, and the students who make them.

Art here is not an afternoon apart from the rest of school life. It is on the floor of the art room with a banner half-painted, on the tables of an exhibition being read by the students who made it, and in the masks and sets that end up under the stage lights.

Work comes from every part of the school, from the junior grades to the senior years. This page collects some of it — the finished pieces, the making of them, and the exhibitions where they were shown.

In the studio

How the Work Gets Made.

Painting, craft and the stage: three kinds of making that go on in the art room.

01 Painting & drawing

On the floor, at full size

The largest pieces are made on the floor, where there is room for them: banners, backdrops and collaborative paintings worked on by several students at once.

02 Craft & handwork

Handwork, on display

Craft is taught alongside painting — work made by hand, finished with care, and put out on the table for the school to see.

03 For the stage

Masks and making

Some of the art room’s work is made to be performed: masks, props and pieces of set that leave the studio for the school’s stage.
