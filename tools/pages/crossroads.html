What it is

A Magazine the Students Run.

The Crossroads Monthly is a student-run initiative to foster journalistic talent amongst the students of CIRS.

We, as a student-centric club, want to empower the students of our school. We want not only our students but even our alumni and parents of students to contribute toward this goal. Furthermore, we would accept all contributions from all kinds of sources provided the content is appropriate and student friendly.
