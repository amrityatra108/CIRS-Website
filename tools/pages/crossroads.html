What it is

A magazine the students run.

As a club run completely by student writers, designers, and photographers, we seek to find this creative spark in every student, latent or expressed. We wish to kindle latent fires and stoke the flames in those who have begun their journey.

Students lead the magazine, while alumni, parents and members of the wider CIRS community may also contribute work suitable for publication.
