Academic journey

Learning changes shape as students grow.

Junior School covers Grades V to VIII; Senior School begins in Grade IX. From Grade XI, students choose between CBSE and the IB Diploma.

  1. V–VIII

    Junior School

    A broad beginning

    From Grades V to VIII, students study Science, Mathematics, English, two other Indian languages and AI alongside the wider Junior School experience.

  2. IX–X

    Senior School

    Towards the first board year

    CBSE continues through Grade X, when students sit the first of the school’s two CBSE board examinations.

  3. XI–XII

    Final two years

    A choice of pathway

    Students continue with CBSE or take the two-year IB Diploma Programme on the same residential campus.

CBSE

The national pathway, Grade V to XII.

CIRS is affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education, New Delhi. English is the medium of instruction.

X / XII

Two board milestones

Students sit CBSE board examinations in Grades X and XII. From Grade XI, the school describes Engineering, Medicine and Management as its three senior streams. Families should confirm current subjects and combinations.

School confirmation needed

Confirm the subject offering by grade, permitted stream combinations and current CBSE affiliation particulars with the school.

IB Diploma Programme

A second path through the final two years.

CIRS is an IB World School. In Grades XI and XII, students choose six subjects, including two languages and Mathematics, alongside the Extended Essay, Theory of Knowledge and Creativity, Activity, Service.

Students study three or four subjects at Higher Level and complete the Diploma core alongside their chosen subjects.

Learning beyond subjects

Knowledge, service and skill.

Academic study sits within the Chinmaya Vision Programme and the life of a residential school.

Value education runs across every grade alongside the academic timetable. Students study the Bhagavad Geeta and the CCMT Life — An Aradhana books, while spiritual classes and aartis bring that learning into daily life. Morning assembly, yoga, service, arts and sport also sit alongside classroom work.
