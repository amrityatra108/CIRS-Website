Broad for as long as it can be. Deep when it counts.

A student can join in Grade V and leave after Grade XII without changing schools: one faculty, one campus and one set of expectations, carried through eight years rather than handed from school to school.

The early years keep a broad course. Nothing is narrowed until it has to be; in Grade XI a student chooses what to study in depth, and the last two years are spent on it.

  1. Continuity

    Eight years on one campus, from the first year of the Junior School to the Class XII Board examination.

  2. Breadth, then depth

    A broad course to Grade X. Then a CBSE stream, or six IB subjects and the Diploma core.

  3. Values alongside

    The Chinmaya Vision Programme runs across every grade, beside the timetable rather than inside one lesson.

Grade journey

One route to Grade X, two beyond it.

Every student follows the CBSE curriculum from Grade V and sits the CBSE Board examination at the end of Grade X. The choice of pathway covers the final two years only.

Structure

1 stream

A stream (Engineering, Medicine or Management), each built on English and a set of core subjects, studied for two years.

6 subjects and the core

Six subjects from six groups, three or four at Higher Level, alongside the Diploma core: Theory of Knowledge, the Extended Essay and Creativity, Activity, Service.
Subject choice

1 of 3 streams

Choose a stream. The stream sets the core subjects; electives are chosen alongside them.

3–4 at Higher Level

Choose one subject from each group, three or four at Higher Level and the rest at Standard Level.
Assessment

X & XII Board examinations

CBSE Board examinations in Grades X and XII; the Class XII examination closes the senior stream.

45 points in all

Examinations at the end of Grade XII, with coursework assessed during the programme. Each subject is graded 1–7; Theory of Knowledge and the Extended Essay add up to 3 points, for 45 in all.

IB Diploma Programme

The IB’s rules, everywhere

  • Six subjects: one from each of Groups 1 to 5, and an arts subject or, in its place, an additional language, individuals and societies or sciences subject.
  • HL Higher Level goes further and deeper than SL Standard Level. Three or four subjects are taken at HL; both levels are graded 1–7.

The Diploma core

  1. Theory of Knowledge

    Students reflect on how knowledge is made and how we know what we claim to know.

  2. Extended Essay

    Students pursue an independent research question and develop an extended piece of academic writing.

  3. Creativity, Activity, Service

    Experiences across all three strands. Required for the Diploma, but it does not add points to the score.

The timetable is only part of the day.

Academic work sits within the Chinmaya Vision Programme and the life of a residential school. Value education runs across every grade alongside the timetable: students study the Bhagavad Geeta and the CCMT Life — An Aradhana books, and spiritual classes and aartis carry that learning into the day.

Morning assembly, yoga, service, the arts and sport sit alongside classroom work.
