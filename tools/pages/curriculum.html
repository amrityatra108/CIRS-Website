International Baccalaureate, Geneva

The IB Diploma, Grades XI and XII.

The seventh school in India

Six subject groups, and the core

CIRS was the seventh school in India authorised by the International Baccalaureate. The two-year Diploma spans six subject groups — three or four at Higher Level — built around the Extended Essay, Theory of Knowledge and CAS. Every IB teacher is IB-trained, several serve as examiners, and university counselling continues through Naviance from Grade X.
