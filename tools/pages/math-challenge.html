Can the same number be a sum of two positive cubes in two different ways?

Find two pairs of positive integers.

1³ + 12³ = 9³ + 10³ = 1729

A complete solution

  1. 01. The problem

    Find a number that can be expressed as two different sums of two positive cubes.

  2. 02. Build the cubes

    Try the pairs (1, 12) and (9, 10). A cube with side n has volume n³.

  3. 03. Calculate each pair

    1³ = 1 and 12³ = 1728, so their sum is 1729.
    9³ = 729 and 10³ = 1000, so their sum is also 1729.

  4. 04. Verify the equality

    13+123=93+103=1729

    Both pairs contain different positive integers, and both give the same sum.
