The idea

Think beyond numbers.

Mathematics is more than numbers. It is about questioning, discovering patterns, reasoning logically and finding creative solutions.

A challenge problem is not a test of what was taught last week. It is set so that the method is not obvious, so that the first idea usually fails, and so that the student who gets there has had to think rather than remember.
