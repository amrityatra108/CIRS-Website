# Retained Crossroads source artwork

The local typography preview uses live serif text and removes the Issue 32
paper stage at the owner's request. These original assets remain available
for future editorial use; they are not rendered or preloaded by that preview:

- `assets/img/crossroads/title-restored.svg` — original gold cursive title.
- `assets/img/crossroads/issue-32-inside.webp` — genuine contents page crop.

The approved feather, opening film, poster, issue covers and PDF archive are
unchanged. Removing the feature stage does not remove any magazine PDF.
