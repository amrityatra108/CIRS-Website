# Houses editorial media provenance

Current page copy and accessible captions: `tools/houses.py`. Crop coordinates and dimensions: `tools/make-houses.py`, `EDITORIAL_PHOTOS`. Run `python tools/make-houses.py --editorial` to reproduce derivatives. No colour grading, retouching or invented dates are applied.

Supplied originals were selected from the school photograph folder and the user-provided ZIP. House attribution rests on visible named shirts, placards or banners, not colour alone. Theatre gallery images retain the documented school production mappings in `tools/theatre.py`.
