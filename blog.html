The brief, to every writer

Stories, ideas and perspectives from the CIRS community.

Student writing, school experience, argument and reflection — the things that happen here, set down by the students, staff and alumni they happened to. The Crossroads magazine has carried that work in print since the school opened; this is where it runs between issues, at the pace of the campus rather than the pace of a print run.
