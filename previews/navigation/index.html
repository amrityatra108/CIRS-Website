The header, the menu and Enquire

An isolated study of the navigation in the approved ivory, charcoal and gold. Nothing here replaces the production menu until it is approved.

  • Menu opens the full-screen menu. Hover or arrow through the five categories; their pages appear on the right.
  • Enquire opens on hover, click or keyboard focus; Esc closes either.
  • The bar stays put while the page scrolls, and opening or closing the menu keeps your place on the page.

Viewing as: Home Founder Our Sports CIRS Theatre News

This page exists so the header can be seen over something. It carries a photograph so that the solid ivory header can be judged against the school's own pictures as well as against paper, and it is long enough that opening the menu partway down can be checked for keeping your place.

Every destination in the menu and in Enquire is the one production uses today, in the same order. Following one leaves the prototype for the live page.
