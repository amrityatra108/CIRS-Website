The creative process

From a thought to a story.

The same five stages whether the piece is a poem or an essay, and whether it takes an evening or a term.

  1. 01

    Imagine

    Let the first idea arrive, before it is any good.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Follow the curiosity wherever it leads, and read.

  3. 03

    Write

    Give the idea a voice. A first draft is allowed to be one.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Shape the words, and cut the ones doing no work.

  5. 05

    Share

    Let the story find its reader.
