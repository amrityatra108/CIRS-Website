Echoes of Yesterday

Poem 01 of 03Opening line

A bunch of thoughts, encircling my mind

By Disha Priyen Bhimani

A bunch of thoughts, encircling my mind
Containing loud echoes of ideas, that I couldn’t find
From the echo of a bouncing basketball,
To the echo of a distant valley’s waterfall

The echoes of yesterday
Remind me of the tournament day.
Where the grief of our defeat,
Echoed through a hundred feet.

These echoes of yesterday are too heard to be forgotten,
But the hope is not to be disheartened
Because this can be
The echoes of tomorrow

As this can be the echo of tomorrow,
Not one of complete sorrow,
With this echo I shine bright
With my mind now ready to fight

Poem 02 of 03Opening line

The wind still hums your half-sung tune,

By Aayush Sreenath Bhashyam

The wind still hums your half-sung tune,
Through trembling blinds and ghostlit noon.
Where silence hums like something torn,
And dreams grow old but never mourn

The mirror hums with faded light
Its glass still holds the edge of night
The face returns, then slips away -
A ghost that never learnt to stay

The clocks all choke on ticking grief,
Their hands like prayers without belief.
And every dusk, the shadows wane,
But whisper back your name again.

Now every night, I drift astray,
Through ruins where your echoes lay -
And though the dawn still finds my eyes,
I live where your tomorrow dies

Poem 03 of 03Opening line

Was it yesterday,

By Utkarsh Shah

Was it yesterday,
I realized,
I was born,
From the heavens without being torn?

Was it yesterday, I used to weep “Stomach Pain”
To bunk school, achieving no gain?
But I did go to school, angry and sad
But so did I return daily, as a happy lad

Was it yesterday, when we used to spend every moment
With family and friends at home
Now we do long for such moments,
In a home away from home

Well, what are these,
These are the echoes of Yesterday,
Which we long to hear,
Even today!!

Poem 01 of 03Opening line

There’s beauty in the sky

By Avika Mundra

There’s beauty in the sky
And in worlds gone by
In the vast, indelible ocean
In all its mystic motion

But those things rarely satisfy
The thirst in those eyes
That only sparkle and shine
At everything between the lines

At the steam from a morn’s cup,
Waking the weary soul up,
A silver thread in a weathered coat,
A lingering song from a distant throat,

Oh incomparable is the beauty
Hidden under the glamour of “pretty”

Poem 02 of 03Opening line

Rain drumming on the dorm roof at night,

By Aryaa Rushab Shah

Rain drumming on the dorm roof at night,
Midnight chai snuck from the mess, shared quiet.
Nailing that math quiz, mates cheering low,
Fist-bumps under the study lamp's glow.

Warden's rare smile, "Lights out soon,"
Group study hacks, laughing through the grind.
Dawn fog on the field, first bell's ring—
These small hostel wins? They keep me alive.

Sneaky card games post-lights out,
Whispers and giggles till someone snores.
Package from home—crisps and a note,
Tastes like freedom behind closed doors.

Sunday night roll call skipped in bed,
Foggy field walks, breath fogging the air.
Stars peeking through the hostel window,
Little lights proving everything's alright

Poem 03 of 03Opening line

The joy of little things

By Anushya UK

The joy of little things
is not waking up happy every day,
but finding small reasons to smile anyway.

Like getting an extra chocolate at the bottom of the bag,
or when your favorite song plays
right when you needed it.

It’s laughing over dumb jokes
that don’t even make sense later,
and sitting quietly with someone
enjoying the silence.

It’s the smell of rain,
the feeling when warm sunlight peeps in through the window,
and watching the same old movies
because it somehow feels comfortable.

We keep thinking joy has to be big,
like something special or planned,
but most of the time
it’s just hidden in simple moments.

And maybe that’s why it matters—
because the little things,
don’t try to impress you.
They just show up.

Turning a New Page

Back to index ↗
Poem 01 of 03Opening line

As the lonely boy lies curled against the wall,

By Tanmay Mehul Khakharia

As the lonely boy lies curled against the wall,
Hoping for a respite from the pain,
The thought that keeps him going, a light in the suffocating dark:
The freedom of turning a new page.

As the writer crumples the upteenth page of words,
Compelled to give through and escape the despair,
The thought that drives his pen across the page:
The fulfillment of turning a new page.

As the woman crosses the war-ravaged fields,
Reaching for the safety beyond the booming weapons of hell,
The thought that pushes her broken, bloodied legs:
The hope of turning a new page.

Poem 02 of 03Opening line

I turned a page today

By Ananda Priyan

I turned a page today
or something like it.
The corner folded clean,
no trace of what it held.

The words behave themselves now,
sit neatly in their lines.
No sudden slips of meaning,
no ink that runs too far.

Still, sometimes in the margin,
there’s pressure without a mark
as if a thought once rested there
and chose not to remain.

It’s nothing you could point to,
nothing you could prove.
Just how the paper softens
where it’s been held before.

I read it like it’s ordinary.
I let the sentences pass.
But something in the silence
lingers just a little longer.

I close the book or so I say,
But some pages don’t turn away.

Poem 03 of 03Opening line

Between the last word and the first,

By Aayush Sreenath Bhashyam

Between the last word and the first,
a breath the universe holds long.
The self, unmade, unquenched by thirst,
becomes the silence before song.
What burned was not the whole of you,
only the name you wore too tight.
The page turns white the way dawn's dew
dissolves into arriving light.
Write nothing yet.
Just feel the blankness.
Even the ocean stills before it broke.
Some depths are born only from darkness
some flames rise only after smoke.

Refuge from Reality

Back to index ↗
Poem 01 of 03Opening line

Where I reside right now,

By Kavya Arun Prasad

Where I reside right now,
Is not where I want to be.
Scared and alone,
In a world not as care-free.

Responsibilities and worries,
Exist as live entities.
Troubling my mind constantly,
Sometimes getting me on my knees.

And I yearn with my heart,
A place where I can sleep.
The softness and warmth of the security,
A long rest without a peep.

So I go running back,
Blind, without clarity.
To my family, a warmth I’ll forever seek,
The one’s who I call, my refuge from reality.

Poem 02 of 03Opening line

Reality is not something easy

By Adira Chellam P

Reality is not something easy
It might make you go crazy
If I had to take a break from mine
I’ll run to the place which’ll make me shine.

Anytime to escape this toxic danger,
I neither have to meet my friends nor a stranger.
I just have to rush towards that room
Where my dance skills will unleash and bloom.

Its a place where I forget everything
I dance to my fullest and sometimes also sing.
This is my refuge, my way out,
I’ll dance forever with no doubt.

Poem 03 of 03Opening line

When the whole world gets way too loud,

By Tarushi agarwal

When the whole world gets way too loud,
And I just hate being in the crowd,
I open my drawer and grab a page,
To let go of all my stress and rage.

With a HB pencil in my hand,
I create my own quiet land.
No marks, no tests, no one to judge,
Just black lines and a charcoal smudge.

This notebook is my silent space,
My favorite little hiding place.
Here I don't have to explain a thing,
I just draw until my thoughts take wing.

Poem 01 of 03Opening line

Bound in chains, held up tight

By Jishnu Teja Yogesh

Bound in chains, held up tight
The world held its reins, denied me light.
Each passing second carved me thin
Until I had nothing more within

It broke through me, shattered my fame
It got me down, seized my flame
No light, not a ray
Just an evil bark engulfed in dismay

But when it all vanished aghast
Rose a faith to flabbergast
And when the end remained face-to-face
The flame it lit, burnt ablaze.

No force remained to keep me bound
No weight remained to drag me around
The world that ruled, now understands
Its my time and its I who commands…

Poem 02 of 03Opening line

Caring, sharing and supporting your friends;

By Ashmi Agarwal

Caring, sharing and supporting your friends;
That is fine, but it might come to an end.
When you are low and blue,
Your heart feels broken into two,
Friends may or may not be there to fix you.

Maybe once, maybe twice,
You will have people by your side.
But to prepare for the last time,
Learn to be alone.

Push your self, motivate yourself,
Because if not someone else,
You will always have yourself.

Poem 03 of 03Opening line

We all live in a world so full,

By Vrittee Fogla

We all live in a world so full,
All negativities gathered up.
We might go down on the way,
But to stay on track, we must get up.

You might have goals to fulfill,
You can get some help too.
But it will last only,
If you change yourself into someone new.

It is not that no one is there,
They are the sticks that support us,
But the actual strength comes from within,
For which lifting yourself is a must.
