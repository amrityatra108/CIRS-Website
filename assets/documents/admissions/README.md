## Sample papers

The `sample-paper-class-*.pdf` files are the school's sample entrance papers, shown on
the Sample papers page (`tools/pages/sample-papers.html`), which the Sample papers tab on
Admissions leads to. Each is the school's own PDF, unedited, copied from the old site's
archive in `pdf/` and renamed only. A paper's year is shown on the page only where the
paper itself prints one ("for admission to academic year 2014", "2010-2011"); the three
English papers for Classes V-VII print none and come from the folder the school named
"Sample papers - 2013". The Class XI IB General Awareness row is the school's own `GENERAL
AWARENESS SAMPLE PAPER.pdf`. Its text is the Management Studies paper word for word (the two
files differ only in their creation time), which fits the IB outline: its General Awareness
section covers the same business and economy content as the CBSE Management Studies section.
The page says so on that row rather than relabelling the paper.

When the school issues new papers, replace the files here under the same names and update
the sizes and years on the Sample papers page.
