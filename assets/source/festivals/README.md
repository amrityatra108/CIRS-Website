# CIRS Festivals — source photographs

The 56 photographs on the CIRS Festivals page, as they came out of the
school's Google Drive, turned the right way up and resized to 2000px on the
long edge (JPEG, quality 88, 24 MB in all). Like the rest of `assets/source/`,
**nothing here is deployed**: `tools/make-festivals.py` cuts the WebP files the
page uses into `assets/img/festivals/`, and `tools/stage-deploy.py` copies only
those.

## How each photograph's occasion is known

Every file came from a Drive folder named for one festival in one year —
CIRS Studio's own event folders, or the social-media account's per-year
selections. None was placed by its look: a costume, a colour or a crowd is not
evidence of which festival a picture shows, and nothing from the Theatre or
Cultural Gallery pages was reused.

Where the camera's clock was right, its EXIF date agrees with the festival and
the caption gives the day. Where it was wrong — the CRS Sony at Onam 2023 and
Pongal 2026, the DSC camera at Dussehra 2025 and the camera at Holi 2025 — the
caption gives the year only, taken from the folder and its upload dates. Those
dates are on the page's list of things to confirm with the school.

EXIF was stripped when these copies were made, so camera serial numbers and
locations stop here. The original file names below find the true originals in
Drive.

Photographs of young children of staff, and frames where an infant is the
subject, were left out on purpose.

## Adding or replacing one

Put the 2000px copy here under a new name, add its entry to `PHOTOS` in
`tools/festivals.py` with the folder, the original name and how its date is
known, then run:

    python3 tools/make-festivals.py
    python3 tools/build-site.py
