# Founder journey build brief

## Source and preservation contract

The authoritative content is `tools/pages/founder.html` plus the approved images resolved by `tools/founder.py`. Preserve every approved name, date, place, paragraph, quotation, milestone, link, photograph, section order, and shared navigation/footer behavior. Do not invent biographical facts or substitute generated portraits. Missing archive photographs remain small, clearly labelled archival placeholders.

## Visitor journey

The page opens and closes in ordinary document flow. Between them, the historical journey becomes one continuous fixed-camera world travelled by the Chinmaya Amrit Vahini. The route begins vertically, broadens horizontally, turns diagonally only at the spiritual transition, and resolves vertically into the educational legacy and CIRS. Reading occupies roughly two thirds of the travel; physical movement occupies roughly one quarter; turns remain rare.

## Feeling curve

Quiet recognition -> curiosity -> resolve -> ascent -> public momentum -> breadth -> reflection -> inheritance -> calm arrival.

The emotional peak is the transition from the first Jnana Yajna to the formation and forty-year reach of Chinmaya Mission. It receives the longest continuous horizontal leg and the clearest view of the Vahini.

## Tell-someone sentence

"The Founder page becomes a road through Gurudev's life, with the Amrit Vahini carrying you through the dates and turning toward the school his vision became."

## Grammar and signature move

- Grammar: continuous historical world inside a bounded journey, with normal-scroll editorial introduction and conclusion as explicitly required.
- Navigation: a quiet route readout showing the current date/title, position, and clickable waypoint markers.
- Hero device: the existing editorial title portrait, not a second cinematic stage.
- Sequence: one primary ScrollTrigger drives the world, vehicle, route, chapters, wheels, and progress.
- Close: the journey arrives at the student legacy, then releases into the existing photographic "The Vision Lives On" conclusion.
- Signature move: the route is simultaneously the road, the progress record, and the navigation. Its travelled gold segment grows under the Vahini and each passed waypoint remains stamped.

## Visual world

Warm archival editorial drawing on ivory paper: fine route linework, quiet topographic contours, gold wayfinding marks, charcoal type, and real supplied photography. This is not a clay diorama, a card carousel, or a sequence of screens. Gold and ivory are mandated by the CIRS brief and used as a heritage wayfinding system rather than generic luxury decoration.

Locked palette: `#FAF9F3`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F4F0E6`, `#EDE7D8`, `#1F1F1B`, `#C9A961`, `#D8BC7A`, `#8F6E2F`, `#E3DCCB`.

## Layer contract

1. Paper ground and grain: static.
2. Topographic line field: slow world layer.
3. Route, waypoints, dates, and archival fragments: world coordinates.
4. Chapter copy and approved photographs: world coordinates with short opacity handoffs.
5. Vahini, contact shadow, progress/navigation: fixed camera overlay.
6. Shared site header: hidden only while the journey camera is active.

## Device contract

- Desktop: large two-dimensional route with vertical, horizontal, and one meaningful diagonal leg; Vahini centered near 52% / 68%, side view dominant.
- Tablet: same chapters and route logic, compressed coordinates and shorter travel.
- Mobile: dedicated mostly vertical route, short lateral offsets, one milestone at a time, Vahini 72-88vw, no horizontal overflow.
- Reduced motion or JavaScript failure: semantic normal-flow timeline with every item, image, link, and placeholder reachable.

## Authored silence

The ivory field between chapters is intentional. No decorative cards or repeating panels are added. Route marks, contour lines, and chapter photography appear only where they explain place, transition, or legacy.

## Verification targets

1440px, 768px, and 390px; opening, first milestone, horizontal middle, diagonal turn, later sequence, final legacy, mobile opening, mobile milestone, and reduced motion. Also verify reverse scrolling, resize, keyboard focus, header restoration, route progress, wheel reversal, no console errors, no failed local requests, no clipped text, and no horizontal overflow.

The registry is empty, so this first build clears the gate. Its fingerprint will be recorded only after the rebuild passes verification.
