The school library holds a collection of 19,600 books, 40 periodicals and 2 dailies, with a large collection of CDs and DVDs. It has an air-conditioned reading room, and every class up to Class X is digitised and provided with course content.

Medical facilities

The school has a Resident Medical Officer and nurses. The dispensary has boys’ and girls’ wards and a full-fledged dental clinic.
