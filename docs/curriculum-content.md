## Open questions for the school

1. **English A course.** The school page says "English A1", a retired name.
   The IB now offers *Language A: literature* and *Language A: language and
   literature*. The page says "English A" and asks families to check which;
   the link goes to the IB's general curriculum page, not to either course.
2. **Academic year.** No year is shown for the IB list because none is
   confirmed. Ask which intake the list applies to.
3. **ESS at HL.** The IB has offered ESS at HL since 2024. The school lists SL
   only; the page follows the school.
4. **Arts substitutions.** The school page allows a Group 3 or Group 4 subject
   in place of the arts; the IB also allows a language. The page gives the IB
   rule and asks families to check what the CIRS timetable allows.
5. **CBSE electives and current combinations.** Only the 2018–19 list is
   published. Electives are not listed on the page.
6. **Written confirmation of commit `1c29ab3`.** The Business Management SL,
   ITGS and Computer Science changes rest on a confirmation recorded only in a
   commit message. A dated subject matrix from the school would settle it.
7. **Contact.** The page uses info@cirschool.org. If the Academic Office or
   IB Coordinator has its own address, use that instead.
